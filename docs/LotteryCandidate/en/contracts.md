# Contract Structure

## LotteryCandidate

Path: `src/dao/LotteryCandidate.sol`

- Manages users' TON/WTON deposits and tracks individual balances (`_balances`) internally.
- Actual assets are staked in the `DepositManager` under the `LotteryCandidate` contract address.
- **Lottery**: Users participate in rounds by paying a set entry fee (`entryFee`). The winner receives all entry fees of that round into their internal balance (Winner-takes-all).
- **Seigniorage Distribution**: Detects seigniorage (network rewards) generated by calling `SeigManager` and adds it to internal balances proportional to each depositor's current share.
- **Withdrawal Management**: When a user requests a withdrawal, the amount is deducted from their internal balance and requested from the `DepositManager`. The user can receive the actual assets after the withdrawal delay.

### Key Functions

- `depositTON(uint256 tonAmount)`: Deposits TON, converts it to WTON, and increases the user's internal balance.
- `depositWTON(uint256 wtonAmount)`: Directly deposits WTON and increases the user's internal balance.
- `enterLottery()`: Deducts the current round's entry fee from the user's balance and registers them for the lottery.
- `drawWinner()`: Draws a winner for the current round and awards the Prize Pool to the winner's internal balance (Operator only).
- `updateSeigniorage()`: Refreshes seigniorage from `SeigManager` and distributes the increased Coinage amount proportionally to all depositors.
- `setEntryFee(uint256 fee)`: Sets the entry fee to be applied starting from the next round (Operator only).
- `requestWithdrawal(uint256 amount)`: Requests a withdrawal from the internal balance. Actual funds are available after the `DepositManager`'s withdrawal delay.
- `processWithdrawal(uint256 n)`: Processes completed withdrawal requests and transfers actual WTON to the users.
- `balanceOf(address account)`: Returns the current internal balance of a user.
- `totalStaked()`: Returns the total supply of Coinage currently staked on this candidate.
- `operator()`: Returns the `LotteryCandidate` contract itself for integration with `SeigManager`. The actual operational authority is held by the `candidate` address.

## LotteryCandidateFactory

Path: `src/dao/factory/LotteryCandidateFactory.sol`

- Manages the `defaultEntryFee` and sets it as the initial entry fee when deploying a new `LotteryCandidate`.

## DAOCommittee Extension

Path: `src/dao/DAOCommittee_V1.sol`

- Creates a new candidate by calling the Factory through `createLotteryCandidate(string memo)`.
